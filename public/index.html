Risk engine

The stop comes before the size

TradePro sizes every order from the risk you accept — not from how much cash is in the account. Measure the market’s noise, place the stop outside it, then let the distance decide the size.

01

Measure the noise

ATR(14) — the average true range over the last 14 daily bars — says how far the symbol normally moves in a day. Computed from real daily candles.

02

Place the stop at 2× ATR

The protective stop sits two average days away from the entry, so a normal swing doesn’t knock the trade out.

03

Size = risk ÷ distance

Shares are your risk dollars divided by the distance to the stop. A wider stop means fewer shares; the dollars at risk stay the same.

shares = risk $ ÷ (entry − stop)  ·  stop = entry − 2 × ATR(14)
