Algorithmic paper-trading demo

A real SMA(10)/SMA(30) crossover strategy evaluated against live daily price data for AAPL, MSFT, TSLA, NVDA and SPY. When the fast average crosses the slow average, the engine opens or closes a simulated position, sized as a fraction of cash, and logs it below. No real money moves — this account started at $100,000 in simulated cash and every trade you see is computed, not scripted.
